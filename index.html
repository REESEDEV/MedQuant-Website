01 / About MedQuant

Where clinical understanding meets hands-on technology delivery.

Established in 2012 and based in the New York / New Jersey region, MedQuant LLC is an independent healthcare technology practice combining clinical understanding with hands-on EHR and application implementation, integration, and optimization.

MedQuant brings years of practical healthcare technology and EHR experience, including professional experience and training involving Epic and Oracle Health / Cerner PowerChart. This foundation connects clinical operations and workflows with practical implementation and technical delivery.

We align clinical, operational, and financial workflows, including revenue cycle and claims management, plan and configure EHR and healthcare applications, connect systems through healthcare integration and interoperability, and support data migration and conversion, testing and validation, go-live readiness, and optimization. Clinical information architecture, data architecture, and solution architecture guide this work, turning healthcare requirements into maintainable, usable technology.
