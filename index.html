01 / About MedQuant

Where clinical understanding meets architectural thinking.

MedQuant LLC is an independent healthcare technology practice bringing together practical EHR implementation and clinical information architecture.

MedQuant brings years of practical healthcare technology and EHR experience, including professional experience and training involving Epic and Oracle Health / Cerner PowerChart. This foundation informs our work across clinical workflows, implementation, healthcare integration, interoperability, data, testing, and optimization.

We bridge clinical operations and technical delivery, aligning workflows with application implementation, information and data architecture, and software and solution architecture. Our approach connects hands-on implementation with responsible design to support healthcare technology transformation.
