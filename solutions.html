Featured MedQuant solution

CDB Constellation

Clinical Data Bridge

CDB Constellation is a governed healthcare information platform developed by MedQuant to help organizations integrate, normalize, govern, and use clinical and administrative information across complex healthcare environments.

CDB Constellation is not an EHR.

CDB Constellation is working software. The capabilities below describe its high-level scope and should not be read as a claim that every capability is production-ready.

The challenge

Healthcare information is often fragmented across environments.

Organizations work with information spread across clinical and administrative systems. Making it useful and trustworthy requires context, governance, and traceability.

  • Information distributed across EHRs, legacy applications, interfaces, clinical documents, extracts, and downstream systems
  • Inconsistent terminology and data representation
  • Patient and encounter identity challenges
  • Migration processes that need consistent governance and evidence
  • Limited traceability across ingestion, transformation, and downstream use
  • Duplicated analytics and inconsistent downstream interpretation
  • Difficulty creating usable longitudinal clinical information

Platform capabilities

A governed foundation for healthcare information.

Working platform capabilities include support for integration, governance, migration, and downstream information use. Capabilities continue to be evaluated and developed.

Clinical Data Integration

Supports healthcare information arriving through multiple integration approaches and source environments, including public standards such as HL7 and FHIR.

Governed Clinical Information

Brings disparate healthcare information into a consistent and governed clinical information foundation for downstream use.

Migration Governance

Designed to support controlled migration planning, mapping, validation, reconciliation, governance, and traceability.

Clinical Document Intelligence

Provides a governed foundation for clinical documents, versions, attribution, provenance, and downstream use.

Identity & Stewardship

Supports governed approaches to patient, encounter, terminology, and data stewardship.

Interoperability

Supports healthcare integration and information exchange while maintaining clinical context and traceability. HL7 and FHIR are among the standards considered at a high level.

Governance & Traceability

Supports provenance, lineage, policy, governance, stewardship, security, and auditability throughout the healthcare information lifecycle.

Analytics & Clinical Intelligence

Supports governed patient-centered information, analytics, population-level understanding, and clinical intelligence use cases.

Responsible AI

AI is an advisory, assistive, policy-aware, and human-reviewed supporting capability. Human review remains authoritative; CDB Constellation is not an autonomous AI platform.
